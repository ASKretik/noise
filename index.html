How it works

▶

Move, collect, escape

Arrow keys or WASD, one cell per tick, eight ticks a second. Collect every key to open the portal, then reach it. An alien entering your cell ends the run.

~

Terrain cuts both ways

Water halves how far you can be seen — every detection ring visibly shrinks. Rubble stops you but not them. Black boxes erase what they knew about where you went.

●

They hunt as a squad

Every ten to fifteen seconds one alien splits in two. The nearest runs you down while the rest post themselves on your escape routes — including the unlikely ones, because that is how a net closes.

■

The file follows you

Twenty-one habits — how closely you hug walls, how long you pause, how directly you go for a key — persist across every district this profile has played, and decide where aliens are sent next.

★

Coins are not score

Coins buy a stun gun and banked lightning. Score judges the run — speed, collection, stuns — and decays each time you replay a district, so farming fills your wallet without inflating your record.

∞

No model is trained

Both predictor tiers are online counting and sufficient-statistic updates. No gradients, no datasets, no checkpoints. The only neural network is a pretrained one, downloaded at runtime to narrate what the predictor is thinking.

Built for UMC301. Plain ES modules, no dependencies, no build step.

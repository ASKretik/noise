NOISE

The city models its couriers. Collect every cache, reach the dead drop, and try not to become legible.

How it works

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, one cell per tick, eight ticks a second. Collect every cache to unlock the dead drop, then reach it. A patrol entering your cell ends the run.

Cover halves your detection radius while you stand on it. Breaking contact lets an alert lapse — patrols hunt at the live position for 20 ticks, then fall back to where they last saw you.

Your score is your own unpredictability: the Shannon entropy of your visit histogram over the level's twelve districts. The system wins by turning you into signal; you win by staying noise.

The file follows you. A transition matrix over districts learns this map and resets when you leave it. A dossier of twenty habits — wall affinity, pause length, how directly you go for a cache — persists across every level this profile has ever played, and reweights where patrols are sent. Level 1 faces a system that knows nothing about you. Level 6 faces one that has watched five levels of your habits.
